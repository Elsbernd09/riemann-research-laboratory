# Frozen Predictions for Equal-Weight Three-Orbit Search

## Phase 4F

These predictions are frozen before running the large three-orbit signed-moment search.

The search concerns

\[
A_r=A_p+A_q,
\]

where

\[
A_k=c_k^6\sqrt{c_k^2+4},
\]

subject to

\[
p\equiv q\pmod2,
\]

\[
r\not\equiv p\pmod2,
\]

and

\[
r>\max(p,q).
\]

---

## P-TRI-001

Prediction:

The initial finite numerical campaign will not certify an exact equal-weight three-orbit cancellation.

Important:

Failure to certify equality in a finite numerical campaign is not proof that no exact equality exists.

---

## P-TRI-002

Prediction:

The minimum global normalized defect

\[
D(p,q,r)
=
\frac{|A_r-A_p-A_q|}
{A_r+A_p+A_q}
\]

will decrease strongly as the maximum search index increases.

Rationale:

The blind-scale moments satisfy seventh-power-type growth and the pair-sum cloud becomes increasingly dense relative to total moment magnitude.

## P-TRI-003

Prediction:

The parity-local spacing defect

\[
E(p,q)
=
\frac{
|A_{r^\star}-A_p-A_q|
}{
A_{r_+}-A_{r_-}
}
\]

will be more discriminating than the global defect D.

A tiny value of D is not by itself evidence of anomalous additive structure.
