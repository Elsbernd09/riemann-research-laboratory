# Riemann Research Laboratory

## Synthetic Zero Surgery and Stability of RH Diagnostics

This repository is an open-ended mathematical research laboratory studying the quantitative response of Riemann-Hypothesis-related diagnostics to controlled synthetic perturbations of zero configurations.

The project does **not** claim to prove the Riemann Hypothesis.

The initial research question is:

> How does a controlled, functional-equation-symmetric displacement of hypothetical zeros away from the critical line propagate quantitatively through different RH diagnostics?

The program begins with the completed Riemann xi-function, zero symmetries, canonical-product structure, and controlled synthetic zero models.

Candidate observables include:

- Keiper-Li coefficients
- Jensen-polynomial geometry
- logarithmic-derivative observables
- selected explicit-formula observables

The project distinguishes rigorously between:

- KNOWN THEOREM
- KNOWN CONJECTURE
- EQUIVALENT CRITERION
- HEURISTIC
- COMPUTATIONAL OBSERVATION
- PROJECT CONJECTURE
- PROVED IN PROJECT
- FAILED CLAIM

No claim of originality is permitted without a prior-art audit.

No finite numerical experiment is treated as a proof of RH.

## Research philosophy

The repository is designed as a reproducible mathematical research program.

The workflow is:

1. reconstruct known mathematics;
2. validate computational infrastructure;
3. define synthetic zero surgery rigorously;
4. measure diagnostic responses;
5. formulate falsifiable quantitative questions;
6. search for counterexamples;
7. audit prior art;
8. attempt intermediate theorems;
9. independently verify central computations;
10. write only the conclusions supported by the surviving mathematics.
