# Research Constitution

## Project

Riemann Research Laboratory

## Initial Program

Synthetic Zero Surgery and Stability of RH Diagnostics

## Central discipline

The project is benchmarked against professional mathematics, not against high-school work.

Advanced terminology is not evidence of mathematical depth.

Large codebases are not evidence of mathematical depth.

Numerical scale is not evidence of a theorem.

## Core question

How does a controlled, functional-equation-symmetric displacement of hypothetical zeros away from the critical line propagate quantitatively through different RH diagnostics?

## Prohibited research behavior

The project must not:

1. claim to prove or nearly prove RH from numerical data;
2. treat finite verification as a universal result;
3. relabel known mathematics as a project discovery;
4. call a statement a theorem without proof;
5. call a statement original before prior-art investigation;
6. silently discard failed proof attempts;
7. fit spectral objects directly to known zero data and present the fit as an explanation;
8. ignore numerical precision or conditioning;
9. infer asymptotic behavior solely from a visually convincing finite plot;
10. optimize the narrative for prestige rather than mathematical truth.

## Proof adversary questions

For every proposed proof, check:

- assumptions;
- domains;
- convergence;
- uniformity;
- analytic continuation;
- interchange of limits;
- interchange of sums and integrals;
- branch choices;
- singularities and poles;
- boundary terms;
- hidden dependence on RH;
- equivalent-criterion circularity;
- floating-point artifacts;
- truncation artifacts;
- regularization;
- operator domains if operators appear.

## Discovery threshold

An observation becomes a project conjecture only when:

1. the mathematical statement is precise;
2. the experiment is reproducible;
3. alternative numerical implementations agree where appropriate;
4. obvious counterexample families have been tested;
5. the observation survives parameter expansion;
6. prior-art investigation has been performed.

A project conjecture becomes PROVED IN PROJECT only after a complete proof survives adversarial review.

## Publication rule

The final paper will report the strongest result that actually survives.

A negative theorem, counterexample, failed heuristic, computational dataset, algorithm, or precise obstruction is preferable to an exaggerated breakthrough claim.
