# Next Phase X4 — Support and Finite Prime Cutoff Theorem

## Goal

For corrected basis functions \(\phi_i,\phi_j\), prove:

\[
\operatorname{supp}(\phi_j*\phi_i^*)\subset[A_{ij},B_{ij}].
\]

Then the prime sum in:

\[
T_{BL}[\phi_j*\phi_i^*]
\]

is finite:

\[
n\le B_{ij}.
\]

## Required

1. support union theorem for corrected basis;
2. star-support theorem;
3. multiplicative convolution support theorem;
4. finite prime cutoff theorem;
5. entry-specific cutoff table.
