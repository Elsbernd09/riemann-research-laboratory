# Theorem X16.1 — constant-term interval enclosure

Assume:

\[
F(1)\in I_1
\]

and:

\[
\log\pi+\gamma\in K.
\]

Then:

\[
-(\log\pi+\gamma)F(1)\in -K I_1.
\]

## Proof

Since interval multiplication encloses all products of elements of the two intervals, \(KI_1\) encloses:

\[
(\log\pi+\gamma)F(1).
\]

Negating the interval encloses the negative of the product.

Therefore:

\[
-(\log\pi+\gamma)F(1)\in -K I_1.
\]

\[
\square
\]
