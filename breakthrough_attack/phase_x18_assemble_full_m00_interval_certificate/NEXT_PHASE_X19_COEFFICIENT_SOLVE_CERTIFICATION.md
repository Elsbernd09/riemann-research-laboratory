# Next Phase X19 — Coefficient-Solve Certification

## Goal

Certify the correction coefficients in:

\[
\Phi=b-\alpha u_0-\beta u_1.
\]

## Required

Prove, using interval arithmetic, that:

\[
\widehat\Phi(0)=0,
\qquad
\widehat\Phi(1)=0.
\]

## Why this matters

Every single-entry interval depends on the corrected basis.

Without coefficient certification, the \(M_{00}\) certificate remains only a candidate.
