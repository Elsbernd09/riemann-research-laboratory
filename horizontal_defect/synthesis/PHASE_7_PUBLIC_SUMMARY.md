# Phase 7 Public Summary

## Short version

The Phase 7 program studied horizontal zero displacement in RH-adjacent analytic structures.

The central quantity was

\[
\beta-\frac12.
\]

The project found that scalar zero-counting methods are horizontally blind, finite linear Li filters are obstructed, positive radial defect energies are easy to define but hard to connect to arithmetic formulae, and quadratic/finitary models can detect horizontal displacement but remain basis-dependent unless tied to a canonical Weil-compatible structure.

## Honest claim

This project does not prove RH.

It does not claim a new RH criterion.

It does not claim a new Weil criterion.

It develops a careful horizontal-defect research laboratory and identifies the Weil-positive arithmetic bridge as the central remaining obstruction.

## Most important lesson

A serious RH-adjacent method must do two things at once:

1. see horizontal zero displacement;
2. connect that detection to arithmetic-side or Weil-positive structure.

Phase 7 achieved the first locally.

It did not achieve the second.
