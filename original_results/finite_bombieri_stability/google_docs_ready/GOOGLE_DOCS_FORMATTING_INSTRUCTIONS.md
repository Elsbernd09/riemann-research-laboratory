- Margins: 1 inch
- Equations: centered where possible
- Page numbers: bottom right

## Tone

The tone should be:

- rigorous
- honest
- professor-readable
- not hype-driven
- not claiming RH progress
- not claiming novelty before review

This note does not claim novelty.
