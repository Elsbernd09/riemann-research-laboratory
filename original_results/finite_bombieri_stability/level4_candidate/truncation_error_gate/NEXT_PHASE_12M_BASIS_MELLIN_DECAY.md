# Next Phase 12M — Basis Mellin Decay

## Goal

Choose a concrete finite basis class \(\{\phi_j\}\) and prove Mellin decay estimates.

## Needed estimate

For every pair \(i,j\),

\[
A_{ij}(k)\le C_{ij,A}(1+k)^{-A}
\]

with \(A>2\).

## Why this matters

This would imply

\[
\delta_Y^{(m)}\to0
\]

and therefore spectral truncation convergence on the finite test class.

## Boundary

No RH proof.

No Level 4 breakthrough yet.
