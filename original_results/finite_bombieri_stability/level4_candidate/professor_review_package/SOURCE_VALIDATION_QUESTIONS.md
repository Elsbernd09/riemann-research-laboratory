# Source-Validation Questions for Professor Review

## Weil/Bombieri convention questions

1. Is the correct quadratic test function construction

\[
F_g=g*g^*
\]

with

\[
g^*(x)=x^{-1}\overline{g(1/x)}?
\]

2. Is the intended quadratic form

\[
Q_W(g)=T[g*g^*]
\]

or does it differ by a sign or normalization factor?

3. What is the exact admissible function space for \(g\)?

4. Does the explicit formula functional satisfy

\[
T[F^*]=\overline{T[F]}?
\]

5. Does the positivity condition have the sign

\[
Q_W(g)\ge0
\]

or the opposite sign?

6. Is the spectral expression

\[
Q_W(g)=\sum_\rho \widehat g(\rho)\overline{\widehat g(1-\bar\rho)}
\]

valid under the chosen conventions?

## Arithmetic-side questions

7. Is the compact-support prime-tail elimination valid under the exact explicit formula?

8. Is the archimedean reduction valid with the stated logarithmic tail?

9. How should the removable singularity at \(x=1\) be handled rigorously?

10. Is this finite-dimensional detection framework known in the literature?
