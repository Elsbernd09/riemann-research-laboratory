# Source Review Checklist

A useful source or professor response must answer these questions.

## 1. Exact \(F_g\)

What is the exact classical construction of \(F_g\)?

## 2. Exact \(Q_W\)

Is the quadratic form

\[
Q_W(g)=T[F_g]
\]

or

\[
Q_W(g)=-T[F_g]?
\]

## 3. Exact \(B_W\)

What is the bilinear or Hermitian form?

## 4. Exact domain

What is the admissible test-function domain?

## 5. Exact positivity theorem

What is the exact RH equivalence or implication?

## 6. Exact finite approximation

Is there a standard finite approximation map \(P_N\)?

## 7. Source citation

Which theorem, paper, or section contains the definition?
