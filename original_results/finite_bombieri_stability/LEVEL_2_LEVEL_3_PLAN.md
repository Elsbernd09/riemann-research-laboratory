# Level 2 / Level 3 Plan

## Level 2 — Professor-readable research note

Required:

1. exact definitions;
2. one theorem;
3. full proof;
4. examples;
5. limitations;
6. reproducible code;
7. honest relation to RH.

Goal:

A professor can read it and say:

"This is careful, technically mature, and honestly framed."

---

## Level 3 — Expert-interest result

Required:

1. the theorem is not merely a restatement of standard perturbation theory;
2. the Bombieri/Weil specialization is meaningful;
3. the stability certificate is useful;
4. prior-art review does not subsume it;
5. an expert reviewer finds the result interesting.

Goal:

An expert can say:

"This may be a useful finite-computation stability note."

---

## Non-goals

No RH proof.

No RH falsification.

No claim of greatest contribution.

No breakthrough claim before expert review.
