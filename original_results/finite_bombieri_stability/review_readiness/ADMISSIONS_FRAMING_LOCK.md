# Admissions Framing Lock

## Safe framing

This project may be framed as:

A rigorous independent research-training project in finite-dimensional spectral stability, motivated by RH-adjacent Bombieri/Weil-style matrix models, with a professor-readable note and honest boundaries.

## Unsafe framing

Do not frame this as:

1. a proof of RH;
2. a falsification of RH;
3. a breakthrough on RH;
4. one of the greatest RH contributions in decades;
5. guaranteed Princeton admission material;
6. novel analytic number theory before expert review.

## Strong honest sentence

"I built a finite-dimensional stability framework for negative eigenvalue counts in Bombieri-style matrix models, proved a support-perturbation certificate, and prepared the result for expert review while explicitly separating finite computation from RH claims."
