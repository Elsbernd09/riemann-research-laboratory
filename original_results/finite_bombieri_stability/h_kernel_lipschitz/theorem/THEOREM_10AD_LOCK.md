## Main theorem

THEOREM_10AD_1

Under the compact-domain hypotheses,

\[
|H(x',y';t)-H(x,y;t)|
\leq
L_H(U,t)(|x'-x|+|y'-y|).
\]

\[
L_H(U,t)=2|t|B_0M_x+2|t|(B_1M_0+B_0M_y).
\]

## Stability insertion

COROLLARY_10AD_2

The abstract \(L_t(U)\) in the combined-radius support-stability certificate may be replaced by \(L_H(U,t)\).

\[

\[
n_-(M(\widetilde\Gamma,t))
=
n_-(M(\Gamma,t)).
