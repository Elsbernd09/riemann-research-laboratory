# Example Interpretation Lock

## What the examples prove

The examples illustrate the theorem.

They show:

1. the certificate can pass and guarantee stability;
2. the certificate can fail while the count still happens to stay stable;
3. the certificate can fail and the count can flip;
4. non-diagonal Hermitian cases are covered.

## What the examples do not prove

They do not prove RH.

They do not falsify RH.

They do not use zeta zeros.

They do not establish novelty.

They do not verify the zeta case.

## Main lesson

The theorem is a sufficient stability certificate, not a necessary condition.
