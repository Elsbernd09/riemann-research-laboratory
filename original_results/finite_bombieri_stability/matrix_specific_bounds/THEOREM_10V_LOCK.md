# Theorem 10V Lock

## Row-sum radius

\[
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{row}}
=
\max_j\sum_k
\sqrt{m_jm_k}L_t(U)(d_j+d_k).
\]

## Frobenius radius

\[
\mathcal R_F
=
L_t(U)
\left(
\sum_{j,k}m_jm_k(d_j+d_k)^2
\right)^{1/2}.
\]

## Combined minimum radius

\[
\mathcal R_{\min}
=
\min\{\mathcal R_F,\mathcal R_{\mathrm{row}}\}.
\]

## Certificate

If

\[
\mathcal R_{\min}<g_0(M(\Gamma,t)),
\]

then

\[
n_-(M(\widetilde\Gamma,t))
=
n_-(M(\Gamma,t)).
\]
