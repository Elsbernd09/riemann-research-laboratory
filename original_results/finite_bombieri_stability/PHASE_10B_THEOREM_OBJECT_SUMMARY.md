# Phase 10B Theorem Object Summary

## We are studying

Finite Hermitian matrices of the form

\[
\mathcal M(\Gamma,t)=D^{1/2}A(t)D^{1/2}.
\]

## We are not studying yet

The infinite zeta Weil form.

The zeta zero limit.

Bombieri Sections 10 and 11.

The truth of RH.

## The first theorem will be

A perturbation-stability theorem for the negative eigenvalue count of this finite Hermitian matrix class.

## Why it matters

Finite RH-adjacent computations can be misleading if small kernel, normalization, or input errors change the negative eigenvalue count.

The proposed theorem gives a certificate for when this count is stable.
