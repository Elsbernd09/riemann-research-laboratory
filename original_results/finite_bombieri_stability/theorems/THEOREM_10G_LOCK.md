# Theorem 10G Lock

## Main theorem

For finite normalized Bombieri-style matrices

\[
M=D^{1/2}AD^{1/2},
\qquad
\widetilde M=D^{1/2}\widetilde A D^{1/2},
\]

if:

1. \(M\) and \(\widetilde M\) are Hermitian;
2. \(g_0(M)>0\);
3. \(|\widetilde M_{jk}-M_{jk}|\leq\varepsilon\);
4. \(N\varepsilon<g_0(M)\);

then

\[
n_-(\widetilde M)=n_-(M).
\]

---

# Kernel-level sufficient certificate

If

\[
|\widetilde H_t(\alpha_j,\alpha_k)-H_t(\alpha_j,\alpha_k)|\leq\eta
\]

and

\[
m_{\max}=\max_jm_j,
\]

then

\[
Nm_{\max}\eta<g_0(M)
\]

implies

\[
n_-(\widetilde M)=n_-(M).
\]

---

# Boundary

This theorem is finite-dimensional.

This theorem does not prove RH.

This theorem does not falsify RH.

This theorem does not use zeta zeros.

This theorem does not claim novelty yet.
