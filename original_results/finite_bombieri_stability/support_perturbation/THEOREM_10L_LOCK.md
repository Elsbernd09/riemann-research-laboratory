# Theorem 10L Lock

## Locked support-perturbation theorem

If the support perturbation satisfies

\[
|\widetilde\alpha_j-\alpha_j|\leq\delta
\]

and the kernel satisfies the local Lipschitz bound

\[
|H_t(x',y')-H_t(x,y)|
\leq
L_t(U)(|x'-x|+|y'-y|),
\]

then

\[
2Nm_{\max}L_t(U)\delta<g_0(M(\Gamma,t))
\]

implies

\[
n_-(M(\widetilde\Gamma,t))
=
n_-(M(\Gamma,t)).
\]

---

# Equivalent displacement certificate

A sufficient condition is

\[
\delta
<
\frac{g_0(M(\Gamma,t))}
{2Nm_{\max}L_t(U)}.
\]

---
