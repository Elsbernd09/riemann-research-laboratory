# Phase 10F Proof Audit Notes

## What was proven

Phase 10F proves that if a Hermitian perturbation is smaller than the zero spectral gap, then negative eigenvalue count is unchanged.

The entrywise version is:

\[
N\varepsilon<g_0(M)
\implies
n_-(M+E)=n_-(M).
\]

## Assumptions

1. \(M\) is Hermitian.
2. \(E\) is Hermitian.
3. The zero spectral gap \(g_0(M)\) is positive.
4. The perturbation is bounded in operator norm, or entrywise through the Phase 10E estimate.

## What was not proven

No zeta-zero statement was proven.

No RH statement was proven.

No novelty statement was proven.

No infinite-dimensional limiting theorem was proven.

## Status

This is a standard finite-dimensional spectral perturbation result, specialized as a stability certificate for the finite matrix branch.
