# Spectral-Gap Stability Lock

## Locked theorem component

For Hermitian matrices \(M,E\in\mathbb C^{N\times N}\),

\[
\|E\|_2<g_0(M)
\]

implies

\[
n_-(M+E)=n_-(M).
\]

---

# Entrywise version

If

\[
|E_{jk}|\leq\varepsilon
\]

for all \(j,k\), then

\[
N\varepsilon<g_0(M)
\]

implies

\[
n_-(M+E)=n_-(M).
\]

---

# Why this matters

The theorem converts a computable entrywise error estimate into a certified invariant negative eigenvalue count.

This is the mathematical core of the finite stability certificate.
