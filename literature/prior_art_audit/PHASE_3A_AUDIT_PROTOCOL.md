## Purpose

The project has developed:

1. a quarter-turn horizontal-versus-vertical finite zero-configuration contrast;
2. a quadratic susceptibility
   \[
   \chi_f(\gamma)
   =
   2\Re f''(1/2+i\gamma);
   \]

# Evidence grades

## PA-0 — Irrelevant

Keyword overlap without mathematical overlap.

## PA-1 — Background ancestor

Contains the standard underlying mechanism but not the project's RH-related application.

## PA-2 — Conceptual overlap

Contains a related sensitivity, perturbation, or comparative scaling idea.

## PA-3 — Strong structural overlap

Contains a materially similar central construction, response quantity, or scaling framework.

## PA-4 — Subsuming prior art

Contains a project claim in materially equivalent form.

A PA-4 result requires claim downgrade or reclassification.

---

# Source preference

The technical audit should prioritize:

1. original research papers;
2. journal versions;
3. arXiv papers where no journal version is available;
4. monographs or authoritative reference works for terminology and provenance.

Secondary summaries may be used for discovery but not as the principal evidence for a technical originality conclusion.

---

# No-moving-goalposts rule

These seven questions are frozen before literature conclusions are recorded.

A negative originality result must not be avoided by silently redefining the project's claimed contribution.

Any revised research question must be recorded as a new question with a new identifier.
