# Terminology Risk Register

## "Mesoscopic"

Risk:

The term has established meanings in the literature on mesoscopic statistics and linear statistics of zeta and random-matrix zeros.

Project use:

The project has used "mesoscopic scaling" for parameter-height coupled limits such as

\[
n/\gamma\to c
\]

and

\[
L\gamma\to c.
\]

Audit requirement:

Determine whether this terminology is misleading relative to established mesoscopic zero-statistics usage.

Possible disposition:

Rename to "coupled similarity scaling" or "parameter-height similarity scaling."

---

## "Susceptibility"

Risk:

Widely used in physics, dynamical systems, and response theory.

Project use:

\[
\chi_f(\gamma)
=
2\Re f''
\left(
\frac12+i\gamma
\right).
\]

Audit requirement:

Determine whether "susceptibility" has a prior technical use for zero or spectral perturbations close enough to create confusion.

Possible disposition:

Retain with qualification, such as "quadratic zero-orbit susceptibility," or replace with "quadratic orbit response coefficient."

---

## "Scaling signature"

Risk:

Generic terminology across asymptotic analysis, machine learning, physics, and renormalization.

Project use:

A family descriptor based provisionally on

\[
(\Phi,\alpha,\Psi)
\]

plus profile-degeneracy information.

Audit requirement:

Determine closest mathematical classification language.

Possible disposition:

Replace with "asymptotic response data" if "signature" suggests unjustified invariance or canonicality.

---

## "Similarity coordinate"

Risk:

Established terminology in similarity analysis and PDE asymptotics.

Project use:

A dimensionless parameter-height combination controlling a scaled susceptibility profile.

Audit requirement:

Determine whether existing similarity-variable terminology already captures the idea.

Possible disposition:

Use "dimensionless scaling variable" unless a stronger canonical property is proved.

---

## "Zero surgery"

Risk:

Could imply an actual admissible deformation of an entire or arithmetic function.

Project use:

Early formal manipulation of finite zero configurations.

Audit result already internal:

The project does not construct an arithmetic deformation of xi.

Current disposition:

Avoid as a theorem-level phrase.

Prefer "finite zero-configuration perturbation" or "finite orbit response model."
