# Q4 Google Scholar Preliminary Disposition

## Campaign

Q4-GS-001

## Question

Has the response of Li coefficients to hypothetical zero displacement already been studied?

## Preliminary conclusion

Broad zero-location sensitivity of Li and generalized Li coefficients is established prior-art territory.

The literature reviewed studies:

- the zero-side Li kernel;
- off-critical zeros and Li-coefficient behavior;
- generalized Li criteria for multisets of zeros;
- reflection pairing of zeros;
- t-Li or tau-Li coefficients tied to zero-free half-planes;
- critical-line phase and Chebyshev representations of Li coefficients.

Therefore the project withdraws any broad implication that connecting Li coefficients to zero location is new.

The narrower unresolved question is:

> Has the symmetry-preserving second variation of the fixed Li zero kernel under finite zero-orbit displacement, together with the coupled n/gamma scaling of that response, been studied?

No PA-4 source establishing a materially equivalent construction has yet been identified in campaign Q4-GS-001.

This is a preliminary Google Scholar disposition, not a complete current-literature novelty conclusion.
