# PA-SOURCE-0006 — Voros

Citation:

André Voros,
"Sharpenings of Li's criterion for the Riemann Hypothesis,"
Math. Phys. Anal. Geom. 9 (2006), 53-63.

Grade:

PA-3.

## Overlap

Voros studies the exact Li zero kernel and derives large-n asymptotics.

Under RH failure, an off-critical zero contributes an exponentially growing oscillatory term of the form

\[
\left(
\frac{\tau_k+i/2}
{\tau_k-i/2}
\right)^n.
\]

The saddle analysis also produces a location proportional to

\[
n/\tau_k.
\]

Thus coupling between Li index and zero geometry is established prior-art territory.

## Difference

The Voros regime studies large n with actual off-critical zeros and their exponentially growing contributions.

The project studies the second zero-coordinate variation at a critical-line orbit and a simultaneous limit

\[
n,\gamma\to\infty,
\qquad
n/\gamma\to c.
\]

No project PA-4 trigger was identified.

## Disposition

CITE AS MAJOR ASYMPTOTIC ANCESTOR.

Withdraw any claim that coupling Li index to zero height is project-originating.

# PA-SOURCE-0007 — Freitas

Citation:

Pedro Freitas,
"A Li-type criterion for zero-free half-planes of Riemann's zeta function,"
J. London Math. Soc. 73(2) (2006), 399-414.

Grade:

PA-3.

## Overlap

Freitas defines a continuous parameter family of Li-type coefficients.

Reflection-related zero terms are paired.

Freitas differentiates the coefficient family with respect to the parameter tau and derives the differential system

\[
\frac{\tau}{n}\alpha_n'(\tau)
+
\frac{n+1}{n}\alpha_n(\tau)
=
\alpha_{n+1}(\tau).
\]

Freitas also derives Taylor representations in tau.

Thus continuous parameter-response analysis for Li-type coefficient families is established prior art.

## Difference

The differentiated parameter is the criterion/evaluation parameter tau.

The project differentiates the geometric coordinates of a finite zero configuration.

No second variation in horizontal zero location was identified.

No H-versus-V contrast was identified.

No project PA-4 trigger was identified.

## Disposition

CITE AS PARAMETER-RESPONSE ANCESTOR.

The project must say zero-coordinate response, not generic Li sensitivity.

Grade:

PA-3 — VERY STRONG STRUCTURAL OVERLAP.

\[

\[
1/2\pm a\pm i\gamma.

\[

No second zero-coordinate variation, H-versus-V quarter-turn contrast, susceptibility 2 Re f_n'', or coupled n/gamma response profile was identified.

## Disposition
