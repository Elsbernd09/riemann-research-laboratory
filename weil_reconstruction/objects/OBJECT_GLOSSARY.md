# Object Glossary

## Nontrivial zero

A zero

\[
\rho=\beta+i\gamma
\]

of the completed zeta function in the critical strip.

RH asserts

\[
\beta=\frac12
\]

for every nontrivial zero.

---

## Horizontal displacement

\[
x=\beta-\frac12.
\]

This is the quantity Phase 7 tried to detect.

---

## Zero-side statistic

A sum over zeros of the schematic form

\[
\sum_\rho h(\rho).
\]

---

## Test function

An admissible function used in an explicit formula.

Temporary notation:

\[
f\in\mathcal A.
\]

This must eventually be replaced by a precise literature-correct class.

---

## Transform

A Fourier/Mellin-type operation on the test function.

Temporary notation:

\[
\widehat f
\quad\text{or}\quad
\mathcal T f.
\]

---

## Explicit formula

A formula relating zero-side data to prime-side/arithmetic-side data.

---

## Weil quadratic form

A quadratic functional

\[
\mathcal W(f)
\]

whose positivity over an admissible class is classically tied to RH.

---

## Negative direction

An admissible test function \(f\) such that

\[
\mathcal W(f)<0.
\]

---

## Finite-rank subspace

A finite-dimensional test space

\[
V_m\subset\mathcal A.
\]

The restriction of \(\mathcal W\) to \(V_m\) may be studied computationally only after the correct \(\mathcal W\) and \(\mathcal A\) are specified.

---

## Toy matrix

A diagnostic finite matrix inspired by Weil positivity.

A toy matrix is not a Weil quadratic form unless an exact connection is proved.
