# Weil Reconstruction Map

## Goal

Create a clean bridge from the Phase 7 horizontal-defect program to classical Weil positivity.

---

# 1. Objects to reconstruct

## Zero side

Understand how sums over zeros enter the Weil explicit formula.

## Test functions

Identify the allowed class of test functions and their transforms.

## Quadratic form

Identify the positivity functional whose nonnegativity is equivalent to RH.

## Negative directions

Understand how an off-critical zero would produce a negative direction.

## Finite truncation

Understand what a finite-dimensional approximation would need to preserve.

---

# 2. Questions to answer

## Q1

What exact class of test functions is used in the Weil criterion?

## Q2

How does the zero-side term depend on the location of zeros?

## Q3

\[

\]

enter the criterion?

## Q4

What does it mean for an off-critical zero to create a negative direction?

## Q5

Can finite-rank truncations detect that negative direction?

## Q6

How do Phase 7 toy matrices differ from actual Weil quadratic forms?

---

# 3. Required caution

Do not claim a new Weil criterion.

Do not claim a new RH criterion.

Do not claim RH progress.

Do not confuse diagnostic toy matrices with the classical quadratic form.

Do not use arbitrary basis behavior as evidence of a theorem.

---

# 4. Desired output of Phase 8

A future Phase 8 manuscript section should be able to say:

1. here is the classical Weil positivity framework;
2. here is where Phase 7 toy models resemble it;
3. here is where they fail;
4. here is the finite-rank problem that remains meaningful.
