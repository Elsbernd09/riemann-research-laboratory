# Negative-Direction Mechanism Card

## Critical-line case

If

\[
\rho=\frac12+i\gamma,
\]

then

\[
1-\overline{\rho}=\rho.
\]

Therefore

\[
g(\rho)\overline{g(1-\overline{\rho})}
=
|g(\rho)|^2.
\]

This is nonnegative.

---

## Off-critical case

If

\[
\rho=\beta+i\gamma
\]

with

\[
\beta\ne\frac12,
\]

then

\[
1-\overline{\rho}=1-\beta+i\gamma\ne\rho.
\]

Therefore

\[
g(\rho)\overline{g(1-\overline{\rho})}
\]

is not automatically a square norm.

---

## Classical meaning

In the Bombieri-Weil framework, off-critical zeros can generate negative directions for the quadratic form.

This is a classical mechanism.
