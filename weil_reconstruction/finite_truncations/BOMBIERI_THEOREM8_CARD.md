# Bombieri Theorem 8 Card

## Source

Bombieri, Section 8.

## Theorem type

Known source theorem reconstructed.

## Statement

The number of negative eigenvalues of

\[
H(\Gamma;t)
\]

equals the number of distinct complex conjugate pairs

\[
(\gamma,\overline{\gamma})
\]

in \(\Gamma\).

## Multiplicity warning

Repeated elements of \(\Gamma\) contribute zero eigenvalues.

The source records that the zero-eigenvalue contribution is

\[
\sum_{\gamma}'(m(\gamma)-1).
\]

The remaining nonzero spectrum is represented through the distinct-\(\gamma\) weighted matrix

\[
H(\gamma_j,\gamma_k;t)m(\gamma_k).
\]

## Project use

This theorem explains the finite-dimensional negative-direction count.

## Boundary

This is a classical finite-dimensional source theorem.

It does not prove RH.

It does not replace the Section 10 and Section 11 limiting analysis.
