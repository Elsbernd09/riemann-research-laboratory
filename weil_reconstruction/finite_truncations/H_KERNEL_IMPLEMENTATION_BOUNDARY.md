# H-Kernel Implementation Boundary

## What the next phase may do

The next phase may implement source-derived symbolic functions:

\[
K,\quad K_t,\quad K^*,\quad H.
\]

It may run sanity checks on function values.

## What the next phase may not do

The next phase may not compute eigenvalues.

The next phase may not use zeta zeros.

The next phase may not claim Bombieri-Weil verification.

The next phase may not claim RH progress.

## Why

Theorem 8 validation requires:

1. exact implementation of \(H\);
2. multiplicity handling;
3. Hermitian convention;
4. synthetic finite tests.

These are not yet complete.
