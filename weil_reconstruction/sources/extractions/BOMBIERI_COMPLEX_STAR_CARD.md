# Bombieri Complex Star Card

## Source

Bombieri, Section 3.

## Source content extracted

Bombieri considers a test function \(f\) that is the multiplicative convolution of a function \(g\) with its transpose conjugate \(g^*\).

The source records

\[
f(s)=g(s)g(1-s)
\]

in its notation.

In the RH direction of the proof, Bombieri uses

\[
1-\rho=\overline{\rho}
\]

and obtains

\[
g(\rho)g(1-\rho)=|g(\rho)|^2.
\]

## Locked project identity

For positivity,

\[
(g*g^*_{\mathrm{pos}})(s)
=
g(s)\overline{g(1-\overline{s})}.
\]

Under RH this gives

\[
(g*g^*_{\mathrm{pos}})(\rho)=|g(\rho)|^2.
\]
