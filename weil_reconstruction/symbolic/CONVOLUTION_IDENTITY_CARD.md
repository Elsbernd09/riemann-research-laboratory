# Multiplicative Convolution Identity Card

## Definition

\[
(f*g)(x)
=
\int_0^\infty f(x/y)g(y)\frac{dy}{y}.
\]

## Mellin transform

\[
f(s)
=
\int_0^\infty f(x)x^{s-1}dx.
\]

## Identity

\[
(f*g)(s)=f(s)g(s).
\]

## With involution

If

\[
f^*(x)=x^{-1}f(1/x),
\]

then

\[
f^*(s)=f(1-s)
\]

and

\[
(f*f^*)(s)=f(s)f(1-s).
\]

## Project use

This identity explains why Bombieri's convolution positivity object creates zero-side terms of the form

\[
g(\rho)g(1-\rho).
\]

## Boundary

This is an algebraic identity.

It is not a new theorem.

It is not an RH proof.
