# Positivity Mechanism Card

## Input

Hermitian positivity adjoint:

\[
g^*_{\mathrm{pos}}(x)=x^{-1}\overline{g(1/x)}.
\]

Mellin transform:

\[
g^*_{\mathrm{pos}}(s)=\overline{g(1-\overline{s})}.
\]

Convolution:

\[
F=g*g^*_{\mathrm{pos}}.
\]

Then

\[
F(s)=g(s)\overline{g(1-\overline{s})}.
\]

## Under RH

For every nontrivial zero

\[
\rho=\frac12+i\gamma,
\]

one has

\[
1-\overline{\rho}=\rho.
\]

Therefore

\[
F(\rho)=|g(\rho)|^2.
\]

## Zero-side positivity

Under RH,

\[
\sum_\rho F(\rho)
=
\sum_\rho |g(\rho)|^2
\ge0.
\]

## Boundary

This explains the RH-implies-positivity direction symbolically.

It does not prove the positivity-implies-RH direction.

It does not prove RH.

It is not new.
