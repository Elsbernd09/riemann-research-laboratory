# Off-Critical Failure Mechanism

## Setup

Let

\[
\rho=\beta+i\gamma.
\]

The Hermitian zero-side factor is

\[
g(\rho)\overline{g(1-\overline{\rho})}.
\]

## Critical-line case

If

\[
\beta=\frac12,
\]

then

\[
1-\overline{\rho}=\rho
\]

and the product becomes

\[
|g(\rho)|^2.
\]

## Off-critical case

If

\[
\beta\ne\frac12,
\]

then

\[
1-\overline{\rho}\ne\rho.
\]

The product

\[
g(\rho)\overline{g(1-\overline{\rho})}
\]

is no longer automatically a square norm.

## Meaning

This is the symbolic location where horizontal displacement enters the Bombieri-Weil positivity mechanism.

## Boundary

This is a mechanism note.

It does not construct the negative test function.

The negative-direction construction belongs to the classical theorem and later source extraction.
