# Fourier-Side Danger Note

## Main danger

A Fourier-looking quadratic form is not automatically Bombieri's Fourier-side Weil form.

Until the exact source convention is extracted, the project must not invent:

- transform signs;
- normalization constants;
- basis variables;
- Hilbert spaces;
- matrix entries;
- eigenvalue problems.

## Forbidden language until extraction is complete

Do not call any expression:

- the Bombieri Fourier form;
- the Weil Fourier matrix;
- the classical infinite quadratic form;
- the Section 8 parent form;
- an RH-equivalent Fourier operator.

## Allowed language until extraction is complete

Use:

- extraction target;
- Fourier-side placeholder;
- source-pending quadratic form;
- unimplemented classical object;
- worksheet item.
