# Log-Coordinate Identity Card

## Main change of variables

\[
x=e^u,
\qquad
F(u)=e^{u/2}f(e^u).
\]

## Involution

\[
f^*(x)=x^{-1}f(1/x)
\]

becomes

\[
F^*(u)=F(-u).
\]

## Multiplicative convolution

\[
(f*g)(x)=\int_0^\infty f(x/y)g(y)\frac{dy}{y}
\]

becomes additive convolution:

\[
(F*G)(u)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}F(u-v)G(v)\,dv.
\]

## Hermitian positivity adjoint

\[
g^*_{\mathrm{pos}}(x)=x^{-1}\overline{g(1/x)}
\]

becomes

\[
G^*_{\mathrm{pos}}(u)=\overline{G(-u)}.
\]

## Prime locations

\[
n\mapsto \log n,
\qquad
1/n\mapsto -\log n.
\]

## Boundary

This is an algebraic coordinate bridge.

It is not a finite matrix.

It is not an RH proof.
