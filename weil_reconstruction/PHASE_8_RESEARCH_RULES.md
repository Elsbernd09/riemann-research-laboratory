# Phase 8 Research Rules

## Rule 1

Classical Weil theory is prior art.

Nothing in Phase 8 should claim to invent Weil positivity.

## Rule 2

A toy matrix is not a Weil quadratic form unless the exact connection is proved.

## Rule 3

Finite-rank negative-direction detection is diagnostic unless convergence to the classical criterion is established.

## Rule 4

Horizontal-defect detection is not enough.

The detector must connect to arithmetic-side or Weil-positive structure.

## Rule 5

Every future theorem must be classified as one of:

- known theorem reconstructed;
- project diagnostic theorem;
- obstruction theorem;
- computational observation;
- open problem.

## Rule 6

No RH proof claim.

No RH-progress claim.

No novelty claim without a serious literature audit.
